Common questions

I deleted the app and reinstalled it — where are my habits?

If you were signed in, sign in again and your habits sync right back down. If you weren't, they lived only on your phone, so deleting the app deleted them with it. A device-level backup (iCloud Backup on iOS, Google Drive backup on Android) can still bring them back if it included the app; otherwise they're gone, and we can't recover them — we never had a copy.

Reminders aren't firing.

First, check that EverMama has notification permission in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Notifications → EverMama; Android: Settings → Apps → EverMama → Notifications). If permission is on and reminders still don't fire, check that your phone isn't in a Focus / Do Not Disturb mode that silences this app.

How do I move my habits to a new phone?

Sign in — that's exactly what it's for. Sign in with Apple or Google from Settings on your old phone, then sign in with the same account on the new one, and your habits and completion history follow you over. Sync is optional and free, and your notes to self stay on the phone they were written on. If you'd rather not sign in at all, restoring the new phone from a backup of the old one also carries the app's database across.

Can you add a habit to the library?

Yes — please write to us. The library grows over time and we read every suggestion. Tell us what the habit is, when in the day it fits, and what you think it builds toward in the long run.
