how it works

three steps, once

1

pick something small

Four squats by the crib. Ten pages of the book on your nightstand. Ten minutes with your legs up the wall.

2

tie it to your day

You'll forget a habit on a rough morning, but you'll remember to put the kettle on. Pair the two and the kettle does the remembering.

3

hold to mark it done

Press the little ring for about a second and it fills. Then a line comes up telling you who you're becoming.

phone or wrist

done in one tap

The reminder shows up carrying your habit and your future-me line. Tap done right there, from your phone or from your wrist. You never have to open the app.

  • Busy hands? Push it back half an hour from the same notification.
  • Tapped it by accident? One ↺ puts it back, and a redo waits in case you change your mind again.
  • If a habit goes three days untouched, you get one message asking what's making it hard. Answer it or leave it, and either way it stays quiet for two weeks.
  • On a day with nothing scheduled, the screen just reads a soft day.
