the research

what the research says

small lasts

Twenty years of research keeps landing on the same thing: the habit that lasts is the one small enough to manage on your worst night.

Stanford Behavior Design Lab

anchors work

Tying a new habit to something you already do has a clunky name and one of the most replicated results in behavior science.

Gollwitzer, implementation intentions

picture her clearly

People who can actually see their future self make measurably different choices today.

Hal Hershfield's lab, UCLA

timing matters

Cortisol peaks in the morning, your muscles are strongest late afternoon, and melatonin rises in the evening.

Satchin Panda's lab, Salk Institute

The library follows postpartum activity guidance from ACOG and the WHO. It gives you something short to do today, and leaves the medical advice to your doctor.
