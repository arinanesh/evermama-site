Delete your account

EverMama, published by EverMama · last updated August 15, 2026

Signing in to EverMama is optional. If you did sign in and want your account gone, here are the two ways to do it — one from inside the app, one by email if you've already uninstalled it.

From inside the app

Open Settings, scroll to your account, and tap delete account. Confirm once and it's done — the account and everything synced to it are removed from our servers right away, and the app signs you out.

By email

If the app is already off your phone, write to hello@evermama.app from the address you signed in with (Apple or Google) and say you'd like your account deleted. We'll delete it within seven days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to reply from that same address so we know the request is really yours.

What gets deleted

  • Your account itself — the Apple or Google sign-in link to EverMama.
  • Every habit, cue, schedule, and completion history synced to that account.

What stays

  • The copy on your phone. Deleting the account removes the server-side copy only. Habits and history already on your device stay yours until you delete the app.
  • Notes to self never left the phone in the first place, so there's nothing to delete on our side.
  • Anonymous usage, crash, and install statistics that were never linked to your account (see the privacy policy). These carry a random id, never your account, so there's nothing in them we could trace back to you and pick out.

Questions before you go? Support is the place.
